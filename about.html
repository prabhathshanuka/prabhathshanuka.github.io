HOW IT BEGAN

A desire to see
education change.

Dependabits started from a desire to see meaningful change in the education system. The journey began with exploring STEAM education and the possibilities of learning through practical experiences.

What began as STEAM classes grew into hands-on learning activities and projects that brought together science, mathematics and technology. The aim was to give learners opportunities to explore ideas, experiment and make things—not simply study concepts.

As the initiative developed, Dependabits expanded into international academic programmes, including Pearson Edexcel IGCSE and International A Level. With other educators joining the journey, the academy continues to grow into a broader learning community.
