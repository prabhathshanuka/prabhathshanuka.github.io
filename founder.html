MY JOURNEY

Creating the kind of learning I wanted to see.

Dependabits grew from a simple belief: education should help learners understand the world, explore their interests and create meaningful things — not simply memorise information.

That belief led me to explore STEAM education and begin with practical learning experiences that brought science, mathematics and technology together. Through hands-on activities and projects, students could experiment, ask questions and see ideas come to life.

My experience teaching Pearson Edexcel IGCSE Computer Science and International A Level Information Technology helped shape the next stage of the journey. Dependabits began expanding its academic support, and other educators joined the effort. Today, the academy continues to grow while holding on to the purpose that inspired it.
